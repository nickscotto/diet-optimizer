Meal Planner

Tell it about you and the foods you like. It builds a breakfast, lunch and dinner that hit your protein, calories, vitamins and minerals.

You

Protein is set from lean mass. Your waist and height give a body fat estimate (relative fat mass; typically off by several points, and a pant tag reads about an inch small, which is corrected). Blank uses a weaker estimate from height and weight.

Diet

Filters apply to every food. A food you add below that matches one is left out and the plan says so.
Uses only foods rated low FODMAP, with daily limits on the borderline ones. Foods you add that aren't rated are left out.
Picks the cheapest foods that still meet your targets, using the Aldi prices on file. Foods without a price count as average.

Foods you like

The plan leans toward these and fills nutrient gaps with staples (eggs, salmon, oats, beans, vegetables, fruit…).

Foods you dislike

These never appear in your plan. Picking one removes every Aldi food of that kind.
